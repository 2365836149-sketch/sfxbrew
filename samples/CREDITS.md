# Recorded samples

These recordings are used as raw material by a few SFX Brew presets (war drum, lightning, gong, erhu, arrow, sword clash, footsteps, landing, shield block, campfire, stream, page turn, typing, chest open, monster growl).
All of them are released under **CC0 1.0** (public domain dedication) on Freesound, so they can be
used and redistributed freely, including commercially. Credit is not required, but we list the
recordists here with thanks.

Processing: taken from the Freesound HQ preview (Ogg Vorbis), trimmed to start at the attack,
mixed to mono, peak-normalized to -1 dBFS, faded out, encoded as 128 kbps MP3.
Multi-take files hold several single hits cut from the originals, one per fixed slot
(see `SAMPLES` in presets.js), each normalized separately. Campfire and stream are 5-second segments whose end is
crossfaded into their start so they loop seamlessly.
