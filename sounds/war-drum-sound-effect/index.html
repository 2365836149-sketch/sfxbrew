Free War Drum & Chinese Drum Sound Effects

Chinese drum hits, from a small tanggu to a large war drum: a deep body that drops in pitch, the slap of the skin and a short overtone. Some variations hit twice. Loud and physical, built for moments that should feel like a battle is starting.

Every press of New in the editor brews a different war drum; these 12 are a fixed selection so the links stay the same.

Good for

  • Battle start and army marches
  • Turn changes in strategy games
  • Heavy impacts and stomps
  • Countdowns before a fight

Tweaking tip

Play two large hits a beat apart, then a gong, for a classic battle opening. Open any variant with Edit to change it with sliders.

Generate them from code or an AI agent

The same sounds are available from the command line:

npx sfxbrew drum --count 5 --out assets/sfx/war-drum.wav

Or let Claude Code, Cursor or any MCP client add them to your project, then ask for "war drum sounds":

claude mcp add sfxbrew -- npx -y -p sfxbrew sfxbrew-mcp
