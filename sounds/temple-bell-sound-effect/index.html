Free Temple Bell Sound Effects

Struck bells with inharmonic overtones and a long, fading ring, closer to a temple or shrine bell than a doorbell. The tail is long on purpose: trim it in your engine or lower the decay if you need a shorter notification.

Every press of New in the editor brews a different temple bell; these 12 are a fixed selection so the links stay the same.

Good for

  • Quest complete or objective reached
  • Shrine, temple and save-point interactions
  • Meditation timers and idle-game milestones
  • Day or night transitions

Tweaking tip

Pitch decides the mood: low bells feel solemn, high bells feel like a notification. Open any variant with Edit to change it with sliders.

Generate them from code or an AI agent

The same sounds are available from the command line:

npx sfxbrew bell --count 5 --out assets/sfx/temple-bell.wav

Or let Claude Code, Cursor or any MCP client add them to your project: claude mcp add sfxbrew -- npx -y -p sfxbrew sfxbrew-mcp, then ask for "temple bell sounds".
