Privacy

Last updated: 2026-11-08

Short version

SFX Brew has no accounts and sets no tracking cookies. Sounds and music are generated in your own browser; nothing you make is uploaded to us.

Stored in your browser

A few preferences are saved in your browser's local storage so the site remembers them: your language, which sound categories you have open, and your Lab project. They never leave your device, and clearing your browser data removes them.

Visitor statistics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views. It does not use cookies or fingerprinting and does not track you across sites; we only see totals such as how many people visited a page and from which country.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub receives your IP address when you load a page and may keep it in its logs for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Recordings and fonts

A few sounds play short CC0 recordings served from this site. No third-party fonts or embeds are loaded.

Advertising

There are no ads on the site right now. If we add them, this page will be updated first and you will be asked for consent before any advertising cookies are used.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: email contact@sfxbrew.com, or open an issue on GitHub (github.com/2365836149-sketch/sfxbrew).

Every sound on this site is made in your browser, from synthesis or a few CC0 recordings, and free for commercial use, no attribution required.
