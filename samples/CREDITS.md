# Recorded samples

These recordings are used as raw material by a few SFX Brew presets (war drum, lightning, gong, erhu).
All of them are released under **CC0 1.0** (public domain dedication) on Freesound, so they can be
used and redistributed freely, including commercially. Credit is not required, but we list the
recordists here with thanks.

Processing: taken from the Freesound HQ preview (Ogg Vorbis), trimmed to start at the attack,
mixed to mono, peak-normalized to -1 dBFS, faded out, encoded as 128 kbps MP3.
