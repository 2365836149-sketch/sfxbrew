Free Hit & Punch Sound Effects

Punchy impacts built from three parts: a sharp crack at the moment of contact, a thump that drops in pitch within a few dozen milliseconds, and a short crunch. That fast pitch drop is what makes a hit feel heavy.

Every press of New in the editor brews a different hit; these 12 are a fixed selection so the links stay the same.

Good for

  • Melee hits and punches
  • Taking damage
  • Objects colliding
  • Screen shake moments

Tweaking tip

For critical hits, play a hit and a short whoosh together, or raise the drive. Open any variant with Edit to change it with sliders.

Generate them from code or an AI agent

The same sounds are available from the command line:

npx sfxbrew hit --count 5 --out assets/sfx/hit-punch.wav

Or let Claude Code, Cursor or any MCP client add them to your project, then ask for "hit sounds":

claude mcp add sfxbrew -- npx -y -p sfxbrew sfxbrew-mcp
