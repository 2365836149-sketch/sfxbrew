Free Gong Sound Effects

Two kinds of gong. The large tam-tam has dozens of clashing overtones and a shimmer that swells after the hit. The Chinese opera gong is smaller, and its pitch bends noticeably downward or upward right after it is struck, the sound behind every Peking opera entrance.

Every press of New in the editor brews a different gong; these 12 are a fixed selection so the links stay the same.

Good for

  • Battle start and round announcements
  • Boss entrances
  • Comedic or dramatic beats (opera gong)
  • Martial arts tournament scenes

Tweaking tip

Opera gongs with a rising bend sound playful; falling ones sound like a dramatic entrance. Open any variant with Edit to change it with sliders.

Generate them from code or an AI agent

The same sounds are available from the command line:

npx sfxbrew gong --count 5 --out assets/sfx/gong.wav

Or let Claude Code, Cursor or any MCP client add them to your project, then ask for "gong sounds":

claude mcp add sfxbrew -- npx -y -p sfxbrew sfxbrew-mcp
