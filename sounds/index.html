Eastern & Xianxia

Sword Slash

Sharp, airy blade swings with a thin metallic ring at the end.

Guqin

Single notes of the guqin, the seven-string Chinese zither, made with a physically modelled string rather than a synth tone.

Pipa

The pipa is a pear-shaped Chinese lute with a bright, percussive attack.

Erhu

Single notes of the erhu, the two-string Chinese fiddle, from real recordings: the nasal, voice-like tone and the player's own vibrato.

Wooden Fish

The hollow knock of a muyu, the wooden fish struck by monks while chanting, also known as a temple block.

War Drum

Rolling Chinese war drums built from real recordings of a dagu, the big Chinese bass drum: heavy center strokes, ringing edge strokes and rim shots, arranged into a steady march, a thundering roll that keeps growing, or a call to battle that speeds up until it hammers.

Temple Bell

Most variations are a large temple bell struck by a wooden beam: a soft thud, then a deep tone whose overtones beat slowly against each other as it fades.

Gong

Built from real recordings of a struck gong and a gong roll.

Spell Cast

A rising whoosh topped by a sparkling run of bell notes and a short echo.

Thunder

Brewing, rolling thunder: rumbles arrive at random moments from different distances, the way one thunderclap reaches you along many paths, over a low, wobbling bed.

Lightning

Real recordings of lightning striking close by: the deafening crack and the roar that follows, each variation pitched slightly up or down so it sounds bigger, smaller or further away.

Breakthrough

A low bell, a soft rumble and a rising arpeggio on plucked strings: the sound of reaching a new cultivation realm.

Talisman Burn

Crackling paper catching fire, with a rising tone underneath.

Potion Bubble

A few bubbling blips followed by a clear bell: the moment a potion or pill comes out right.

Wind Chime

A few soft, high notes from metal tubes on the pentatonic scale, each with a faint shimmer from a slightly detuned twin, like chimes moved by a breeze.

Bamboo Flute

Single notes in the style of the dizi, the Chinese bamboo flute: a soft fundamental with breath noise that follows the pitch, a puff at the start, gentle vibrato, and the faint buzz of the dizi's membrane that gives it its reedy edge.

Energy Charge

A swelling, wobbling rise that ends in a bell, like energy gathering before release.

General Game Sounds

Coin

Two-note metallic dings for collecting money.

Item Pickup

Quick upward chirps with a small sparkle on top.

Button Click

Very short tactile ticks for interface buttons: a tiny burst of filtered noise for the contact plus a quick tonal body.

UI Confirm

Two pleasant electric-piano notes going up.

Error

Two dull, low notes made from stacked, slightly detuned voices.

Jump

Short rising boings with a puff of air.

Hit

Punchy impacts built from three parts: a sharp crack at the moment of contact, a thump that drops in pitch within a few dozen milliseconds, and a short crunch.

Explosion

Violent blasts: a sharp shockwave, a sub-bass punch, a saturated body that stays at full force before it fades, a mid-range roar, debris raining down at random and a wobbling aftershock.

Laser

Falling "pew" zaps made with FM synthesis and two slightly detuned voices, so they have bite without the harshness of a raw square wave.

Power-up

Either a quick rising arpeggio or a filtered sweep that swells upward.

Level Up

A bright arpeggio that lands on a soft chord and a bell.

Whoosh

Airy swooshes made from two filtered noise bands sweeping upward.
