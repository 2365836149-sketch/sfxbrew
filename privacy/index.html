Privacy

Last updated: 2026-11-10

Short version

SFX Brew sets no tracking cookies. Sounds and music are generated in your own browser; nothing you make is uploaded to us. The exceptions are the Text to sound effect beta in the Lab and the optional email sign-in for buying credits, both described below.

Text to sound effect (beta)

When you use Lab → Text to sound effect, the description you type (and any change you ask for) is sent to our server, which runs on a machine we operate in Germany and is reached through Cloudflare. To interpret it, the text is passed to DeepSeek, an AI service operated in China; please do not type personal information. The server uses your IP address only in memory to enforce rate limits and forgets it within a day. To improve the tool and to train our own sound models, we keep, without your IP address and for up to 2 years: the text you typed, how the AI interpreted it, the settings of the sounds we generated for you (not the audio itself) and what you did with them on the page (playing, downloading, picking one to refine, thumbs up or down). These records are linked only by a random number created when you open the page and discarded when you close it, so separate visits cannot be connected. If you are signed in, we only note that the request was paid, not who made it. The generated audio files are deleted after 2 hours.

Sign-in and credits

To buy credits you sign in with your email address: we email you a 6-digit code (sent through Resend, our email provider) and, once you enter it, keep a sign-in token in your browser's local storage until you sign out. On our server we store your email address, your credit balance, the Stripe order IDs of your purchases (to handle refunds) and a one-way hash of the sign-in token. Checkout and payment run on Stripe, which acts as the seller (merchant of record) through Stripe Managed Payments and processes your name, email, address and payment details under its own privacy policy; we never see your card details. We use your email address only for signing in and questions about your purchases, never for marketing. To delete your account, email contact@sfxbrew.com from that address.

Bot protection

The Text to sound effect page uses Cloudflare Turnstile to block automated abuse. Turnstile checks your browser when you press Generate and does not set advertising or tracking cookies. See the Cloudflare Turnstile privacy addendum.

Stored in your browser

A few preferences are saved in your browser's local storage so the site remembers them: your language, which sound categories you have open, and your Lab project. They never leave your device, and clearing your browser data removes them.

Visitor statistics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views. It does not use cookies or fingerprinting and does not track you across sites; we only see totals such as how many people visited a page and from which country.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub receives your IP address when you load a page and may keep it in its logs for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Recordings and fonts

A few sounds play short CC0 recordings served from this site. No third-party fonts are loaded; the only third-party embed is Cloudflare Turnstile on the Text to sound effect page.

Advertising

There are no ads on the site right now. If we add them, this page will be updated first and you will be asked for consent before any advertising cookies are used.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: email contact@sfxbrew.com, or open an issue on GitHub (github.com/2365836149-sketch/sfxbrew).

Every sound on this site is made in your browser, from synthesis or a few CC0 recordings, and free for commercial use, no attribution required.
