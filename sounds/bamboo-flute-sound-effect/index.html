Free Bamboo Flute Sound Effects

Single notes in the style of the dizi, the Chinese bamboo flute: a soft fundamental with breath noise that follows the pitch, a puff at the start, gentle vibrato, and the faint buzz of the dizi's membrane that gives it its reedy edge.

Every press of New in the editor brews a different bamboo flute; these 12 are a fixed selection so the links stay the same.

Good for

  • Opening a map, journal or menu
  • Scene transitions
  • Pastoral or village areas
  • Character introductions

Tweaking tip

Less vibrato sounds calmer and more modern; more vibrato sounds older and more expressive. Open any variant with Edit to change it with sliders.

Generate them from code or an AI agent

The same sounds are available from the command line:

npx sfxbrew flute --count 5 --out assets/sfx/bamboo-flute.wav

Or let Claude Code, Cursor or any MCP client add them to your project, then ask for "bamboo flute sounds":

claude mcp add sfxbrew -- npx -y -p sfxbrew sfxbrew-mcp
